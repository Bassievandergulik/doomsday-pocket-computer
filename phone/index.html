Before you start

This works fully offline once it is installed. It uses the phone's gyro, accelerometer, compass and GPS.

  1. Let it align: on foot, lay the phone still, screen up, for about 30 seconds. In a vehicle or boat, put it somewhere it stays put for about a minute (moving is fine).
  2. Keep the app open: phones pause the sensors when the screen locks, so it keeps the screen on.
  3. The heading is the direction the top of the phone points. On foot, hold it pointing the way you go.

      Settings

      The phone compass points to magnetic north. Look up the variation on a chart or map for your area (the Netherlands is about +2°).

      What this can and cannot do

      A phone gyro drifts tens of degrees per hour, so it cannot measure position on its own like an airliner's INS. It keeps a steady heading through turns, rolling and nearby magnets, and checks every GPS fix against what the sensors felt: sudden jumps, speed changes nothing pushed, turns the gyro didn't feel, steps while GPS says you stand still.

      Position without GPS is speed × course (bike, car, boat, plane) or steps × step length (on foot). It does not know the current, wind or speed changes, so the uncertainty grows over time. A slow, steady drag of the GPS position can look like normal movement and is only caught once it exceeds that uncertainty.
