# Choking

## Can they cough or talk?
Yes, mild obstruction: encourage them to keep **coughing**. Stay with them.

## Cannot breathe, cough or speak (adult or child over 1)
1. Stand beside and slightly behind. Support the chest, lean them well forward.
2. Give up to **5 back blows** between the shoulder blades with the heel of your hand. Check after each.

![Back blows](fig:choking-back-blows.svg)

3. Still stuck: give up to **5 abdominal thrusts**: stand behind, fist just above the belly button, other hand over it, pull sharply **inwards and upwards**.

![Abdominal thrusts](fig:choking-thrusts.svg)

4. Alternate 5 back blows and 5 thrusts.

5. They go unconscious: lower them down, call 112, start [CPR](page:firstaid/cpr). Look in the mouth before breaths and remove anything visible.

Anyone who received abdominal thrusts should be checked by a doctor afterwards (internal injury is possible).

## Infant under 1 year
1. Lay the baby face down along your forearm, head lower than the body, supporting the jaw.
2. Up to **5 back blows**.
3. Turn face up along your other arm, head low. Up to **5 chest thrusts** with two fingers on the breastbone (like CPR but sharper and slower).
4. Repeat. **Never** do abdominal thrusts on an infant.

![Infant: back blows along your forearm](fig:choking-infant.svg)

## Alone and choking
Thrust your own belly hard against the back of a chair, table edge or railing.
