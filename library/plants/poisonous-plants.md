# Deadly and poisonous plants

Know these before you pick anything. Most dangerous are the **carrot family** (white umbrella-shaped flower heads): several are deadly and look alike, so **never eat any plant with umbrella flowers** unless you are an expert. If someone ate a poisonous plant: don't make them vomit, keep a sample, call 112 / a poison centre if possible, and watch breathing.

## Hemlock
![Hemlock](photo:hemlock.jpg)

*Conium maculatum* · NL: gevlekte scheerling · ES: cicuta

- **Recognise**: 1–2.5 m, smooth hollow stem with **purple blotches**, finely divided fern-like leaves, white umbrella flowers, mousy smell
- **Effect**: paralysis, death by breathing failure. Even touching sap is unwise

## Hemlock water-dropwort
![Hemlock water-dropwort](photo:hemlock-water-dropwort.jpg)

*Oenanthe crocata* · NL: dodemansvingers · ES: nabo del diablo

- **Recognise**: by water, celery/parsley-like leaves, white umbrella flowers, roots like pale **'dead man's fingers'**, yellow sap
- **Effect**: one of the most poisonous plants in Europe: seizures, death within hours

## Giant hogweed
![Giant hogweed](photo:giant-hogweed.jpg)

*Heracleum mantegazzianum* · NL: reuzenberenklauw · ES: perejil gigante

- **Recognise**: huge, 2–5 m, thick hollow stems with purple spots and bristles, enormous jagged leaves, white umbrella heads up to 50 cm
- **Effect**: sap + sunlight causes severe blisters and scars for years. Cover skin, wash any sap off at once and keep it out of the sun for days

## Foxglove
![Foxglove](photo:foxglove.jpg)

*Digitalis purpurea* · NL: vingerhoedskruid · ES: dedalera

- **Recognise**: tall spike of hanging purple (sometimes white) tube flowers with spots inside; soft hairy leaves in a rosette the year before
- **Effect**: heart poison. Leaves resemble comfrey and borage: don't eat hairy rosette leaves you are unsure of

## Lily of the valley
![Lily of the valley](photo:lily-of-the-valley.jpg)

*Convallaria majalis* · NL: lelietje-van-dalen · ES: lirio de los valles

- **Recognise**: two or three broad leaves wrapped around one stalk, small white **bell** flowers on one side, red berries later; **no garlic smell**
- **Effect**: heart poison. The classic lookalike of wild garlic

## Autumn crocus
![Autumn crocus](photo:autumn-crocus.jpg)

*Colchicum autumnale* · NL: herfsttijloos · ES: cólquico

- **Recognise**: pale purple crocus-like flowers in autumn without leaves; broad tulip-like leaves in spring (looks like wild garlic, no smell)
- **Effect**: colchicine: deadly, symptoms start after hours, no antidote

## Deadly nightshade
![Deadly nightshade](photo:deadly-nightshade.jpg)

*Atropa belladonna* · NL: wolfskers · ES: belladona

- **Recognise**: bushy herb 0.5–1.5 m, dull purple-brown bell flowers, **shiny black cherry-sized berries** sitting in a green star
- **Effect**: a few berries can kill a child: wide pupils, hot dry skin, confusion

## Yew
![Yew](photo:yew.jpg)

*Taxus baccata* · NL: venijnboom · ES: tejo

- **Recognise**: dark evergreen with flat needles in two rows, red fleshy cups around a seed, no cones; common in parks and graveyards
- **Effect**: everything except the red flesh is deadly (the seed inside it too). Don't use as tea, skewers or cooking sticks

## Monkshood
![Monkshood](photo:monkshood.jpg)

*Aconitum napellus* · NL: monnikskap · ES: acónito

- **Recognise**: spikes of dark blue hood-shaped flowers, deeply cut hand-shaped leaves; gardens and damp mountain meadows
- **Effect**: extremely poisonous, even through the skin. Wear gloves if you must touch it

## Lords-and-ladies
![Lords-and-ladies](photo:lords-and-ladies.jpg)

*Arum maculatum* · NL: gevlekte aronskelk · ES: aro

- **Recognise**: arrow-shaped glossy leaves (often black-spotted) in spring woods; in late summer a spike of **bright orange-red berries**
- **Effect**: burning pain and swelling of mouth and throat. Young leaves are mixed up with sorrel and wild garlic

## Horse chestnut
![Horse chestnut](photo:horse-chestnut.jpg)

*Aesculus hippocastanum* · NL: paardenkastanje · ES: castaño de Indias

- **Recognise**: large hand-shaped leaves of 5–7 leaflets, white flower 'candles', round shiny conkers in a husk with few short spikes
- **Effect**: vomiting and diarrhoea. Compare with sweet chestnut on [Edible plants](page:plants/edible-plants)

## Castor bean, oleander, rosary pea
![Castor bean, oleander, rosary pea](photo:castor-bean.jpg)

*Ricinus communis / Nerium oleander / Abrus precatorius* · NL: wonderboom / oleander / paternostererwt · ES: ricino / adelfa / peonía

- **Castor bean**: large star-shaped reddish leaves, spiny seed pods, mottled beans: the seeds contain ricin, deadly when chewed
- **Oleander**: garden shrub (south Europe, Caribbean), narrow leathery leaves, pink/white flowers: all parts deadly, also the smoke
- **Rosary pea**: climbing vine with red seeds with a black spot (used in jewellery): one chewed seed can kill
