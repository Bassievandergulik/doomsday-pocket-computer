# Danger and hazard symbols

The symbols below are the same across Europe and most of the world. When you see one on a container, truck or door: **keep your distance, stay upwind and uphill, and don't touch, smell or taste**.

## Chemical hazard pictograms (red diamond, GHS/CLP)
Found on bottles, cans and drums, including household products.

| Symbol | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| ![Exploding bomb](sign:ghs-explosive.svg) | **Explosive**: can explode from fire, shock or friction. | No fire, no knocking, keep far away. |
| ![Flame](sign:ghs-flammable.svg) | **Flammable**: gas, liquid or solid that burns easily. | No flames or sparks; keep cool and closed. |
| ![Flame over circle](sign:ghs-oxidising.svg) | **Oxidising**: makes fires burn much harder. | Keep away from fuel, wood, cloth. |
| ![Gas cylinder](sign:ghs-gas.svg) | **Gas under pressure**: can burst when heated; cold gas burns skin. | Keep out of sun and heat; never puncture. |
| ![Corrosion](sign:ghs-corrosive.svg) | **Corrosive**: burns skin and eyes, eats metal. | Gloves and eye protection; rinse skin 15 minutes with water. |
| ![Skull and crossbones](sign:ghs-toxic.svg) | **Acutely toxic**: small amounts can kill by swallowing, breathing or skin contact. | Don't touch; never use the container for food or water. |
| ![Exclamation mark](sign:ghs-harmful.svg) | **Harmful / irritant**: irritates skin, eyes or lungs; less severe. | Avoid contact, ventilate. |
| ![Health hazard](sign:ghs-health.svg) | **Serious health hazard**: cancer, lung damage, harm to unborn children. | Avoid breathing it; never use as drinking-water container. |
| ![Environment](sign:ghs-environment.svg) | **Hazardous to the environment**: poisonous to fish and water life. | Keep out of water sources; never pour away. |

> A container that ever held a red-diamond product is **not safe for drinking water**, even after rinsing.

## Other warning signs
| Symbol | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ![Radiation](sign:radiation.svg) | **Radiation** (ionising). Leave the area, don't pick anything up. After a nuclear incident: go inside, close windows, listen to the radio. |
| ![Biohazard](sign:biohazard.svg) | **Biological hazard**: infectious material (hospital waste, labs). Don't touch; wash hands. |
| ![Warning triangle](sign:warning.svg) | **General danger** (yellow triangle). The picture inside tells what: lightning bolt = electricity, flame = fire, skull = poison. |

## Orange plates on trucks, trains and tanks (ADR)
![Orange plate 33 over 1203](sign:adr-33-1203.svg)

The **top number** is the danger, the **bottom number** is the substance (UN number). Tell emergency services both numbers.

| Top digit | Means |
|---|---|
| 2 | gas |
| 3 | flammable liquid |
| 4 | flammable solid |
| 5 | oxidising (feeds fire) |
| 6 | toxic |
| 7 | radioactive |
| 8 | corrosive |
| 9 | other danger (hot material, lithium batteries) |

A **doubled digit** means stronger (33 = highly flammable liquid). An **X** in front (X423) means: **never use water** on it. Common ones: **33/1203** petrol, **30/1202** diesel, **23/1965** LPG, **22/1977** liquid nitrogen, **80/1830** sulphuric acid, **268/1005** ammonia (toxic, corrosive gas).

## Safety sign colours (ISO 7010)
| Colour and shape | Means | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Green** rectangle | safe condition | emergency exit, first aid, AED, assembly point |
| **Red** circle with bar | prohibited | no smoking, no open flame, no entry |
| **Red** square | fire equipment | fire extinguisher, fire hose, fire alarm |
| **Yellow** triangle | warning | electricity, slippery, toxic |
| **Blue** circle | you must | wear mask, wear hearing protection |

**AED** (defibrillator): green sign with a white heart and lightning bolt. Anyone may use one: open it and follow the spoken instructions. See [CPR](page:firstaid/cpr).
