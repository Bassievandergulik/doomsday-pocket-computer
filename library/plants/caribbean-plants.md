# Caribbean plants (Curaçao, Aruba, Bonaire)

The ABC islands are dry with cactus scrub, thorn bush and mangroves. Fresh water and edible plants are scarce, so most food comes from the sea and the few plants below. Papiamentu names are given where known. Heat, sea animals and water: [Heat, desert and tropical islands](page:wild/hot-climates).

## Manchineel: deadly
![Manchineel: deadly](photo:manchineel.jpg)

*Hippomane mancinella* · NL: manzanilla (Pap.: manzaniya) · ES: manzanillo

- **Recognise**: tree on beaches, shiny bright-green oval leaves with finely toothed edges, small green-yellow **apple-like fruits**; often marked with a red band or warning sign
- **Effect**: fruit causes burning, blisters and throat swelling; milky sap blisters skin and can blind; don't shelter under it in rain, don't burn the wood

## Sea grape
![Sea grape](photo:sea-grape.jpg)

*Coccoloba uvifera* · NL: zeedruif (Pap.: druif di laman) · ES: uva de playa

- **Recognise**: beach shrub or tree with very large, round, leathery leaves with red veins; hanging bunches of grape-sized fruits turning purple
- **Eat**: ripe purple fruit raw (large seed); leaves used as plates

## Prickly pear
![Prickly pear](photo:prickly-pear.jpg)

*Opuntia ficus-indica* · NL: vijgcactus (Pap.: tuna) · ES: nopal / tuna

- **Recognise**: cactus made of flat oval pads, yellow flowers, red or yellow egg-shaped fruits
- **Eat**: fruit and young pads. First remove the tiny hair-like spines (glochids): hold with a stick, burn them off or rub hard in sand, then peel. Pads: cook

## Coconut
![Coconut](photo:coconut.jpg)

*Cocos nucifera* · NL: kokospalm · ES: cocotero

- **Recognise**: tall palm with feather leaves and big green or brown nuts
- **Eat**: green nuts: water to drink (very good) and soft jelly; brown nuts: hard white flesh (fat, energy). Don't drink too much milk of ripe nuts (laxative). Never sit under a coconut palm

## Purslane and others
![Purslane and others](photo:purslane.jpg)

*Portulaca oleracea* · NL: postelein · ES: verdolaga

- **Purslane**: grows wild after rain; see [Edible plants](page:plants/edible-plants)
- **Oleander and castor bean**: common in gardens and roadsides on the islands: deadly, see [Poisonous plants](page:plants/poisonous-plants)
