# Heat exhaustion and heatstroke

## Heat exhaustion
Heavy sweating, headache, dizziness, nausea, cramps, fast pulse, temperature up to ~40 °C, still thinking clearly.
1. Move to shade or a cool place, lie down, legs raised.
2. Remove extra clothing.
3. Drink water or an [oral rehydration solution](page:water/how-much) in sips.
4. Cool skin: wet cloths, fanning.
Should improve within 30 minutes. If not, treat as heatstroke.

## Heatstroke (emergency)
Hot skin (dry **or** sweaty), temperature above 40 °C, **confusion, odd behaviour, collapse, seizures**.
1. Call 112.
2. **Cool fast**: best is immersion in cool water up to the neck. Otherwise soak with water and fan hard, ice packs in neck, armpits and groin.
3. Keep cooling until they are clearly better, then watch: it can come back.
4. Unconscious: recovery position, CPR if not breathing normally.

## Prevention
- Avoid hard work between 11:00 and 16:00, rest in shade.
- Drink before thirsty, urine should be pale.
- Light loose clothing, hat. Keep children and elderly cool and check on them.
- Never leave anyone in a parked car.
