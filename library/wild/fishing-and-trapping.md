# Fishing, trapping and preparing game

Fish are the easiest wild protein almost everywhere. Trapping and hunting are illegal in normal times in most of Europe and kill animals slowly when done badly: use them only in a genuine long-term survival situation.

## Fishing
**Where**: in shade, under overhanging banks and trees, behind rocks and in eddies where fast water meets slow, at inflows of streams, near reeds. Early morning and evening are best; in hot weather fish go deep.

**Improvised gear**
- Hook: a safety pin, a bent nail or wire, a thorn (hawthorn, blackthorn), a "gorge" (a small sliver of bone or wood sharpened at both ends, tied in the middle, swallowed lengthwise with the bait).

![Gorge hook](fig:gorge-hook.svg)

- Line: thread from clothing, dental floss, inner strands of paracord, plant fibre ([Tools and cordage](page:wild/tools-and-cordage)).
- Bait: worms, insects, grubs from rotten wood, small fish, fish guts. Bright cloth or foil as a lure.
- **Night lines**: several baited lines tied to branches or a stake, checked morning and evening, catch more than you can by sitting.
- **Fish trap**: a funnel of sticks or a cut plastic bottle with the neck turned inward; or stones laid in a V in a shallow stream with the point downstream, closed off behind.

![Stone V-trap in a stream](fig:stone-trap.svg)

![Bottle trap](fig:bottle-trap.svg)

- Spear: a straight stick split into 4 prongs, wedged apart. Aim below where the fish seems to be (water bends light).

**Preparing**: kill quickly with a blow behind the eyes. Gut within an hour: cut from the vent to the gills, remove everything inside and the dark blood line along the spine. Cook all freshwater fish thoroughly (parasites). Eat soon or dry/smoke it: [Food storage](page:food/storage).

Don't eat fish that smell, have slimy grey gills, sunken eyes or flesh that stays dented when pressed.

## Trapping (only in real need)
Small game paths are visible as narrow trails through grass, to water, or tunnels under fences. Set traps where the path narrows, without disturbing it, and check them at least twice a day.
- **Snare**: a loop of thin wire (fist-sized for rabbits, about 10 cm above the ground) anchored to a stake, on a run. Brass picture wire works best.
- **Deadfall** (figure-4 trigger): a heavy flat stone held up by three notched sticks; dangerous to set. Practise first.
- Never set traps where people, dogs or protected animals pass.

## Preparing small game
1. Kill quickly. Wear gloves if possible; avoid animals that seem sick or act strangely (rabies, tularemia in hares).
2. Cut the skin around the back legs and pull it off like a sock towards the head.
3. Open the belly carefully without cutting the guts; remove all organs. The liver should be dark red and smooth: if it has white spots, throw the animal away.
4. Rinse, cook **thoroughly** until no pink is left. Boiling keeps all the fat and nutrients in the broth.

Rabbit alone does not keep you alive ("rabbit starvation"): you also need fat or carbohydrates.

## Insects and other small food
Grasshoppers, crickets, ant larvae, beetle grubs, earthworms (purged in water) are rich in protein; always cook them. Remove legs and wings of grasshoppers. Avoid caterpillars with hairs and brightly coloured insects. Snails: see [Foraging](page:food/foraging).
