# Specific emergencies

## Power outage (long)
- Turn off sensitive appliances; leave one light switched on to know when power returns.
- Fridge closed, eat fridge food first ([Food](page:food/storage)).
- Heating: dress in layers, live in one room, never use a barbecue or gas heater not made for indoors.
- Water supply may stop after a day or so if pumps lose power: fill bathtub, buckets and bottles early.
- Cash: card payments stop. Keep small notes at home.
- Radio for official information ([Radio guide](page:comms/radio)).

## Flood (very relevant in NL/BE/DE)
- Listen to the regional broadcaster for evacuation orders. If told to evacuate, **go early**: roads become blocked.
- Can't leave: go to the highest floor, take water, food, radio, warm clothes, phone/this device, medicines. Turn off gas and electricity if safe.
- Never walk or drive through moving water: 15 cm can knock you over, 30 cm floats a car. Floodwater is sewage-contaminated: treat cuts, wash hands.
- After: throw away food touched by floodwater, boil drinking water until authorities say otherwise.

## Storm
Secure loose objects, stay indoors away from windows, avoid woods and trees for 24 h after (broken branches fall later), watch for fallen power lines (stay 10 m away).

## Heat wave
See [Heat illness](page:firstaid/heat). Close windows and curtains by day, open at night. Check on elderly neighbours.

## Wildfire
Leave early when told. Car: windows closed, vents on recirculate, headlights on. Trapped: go to a bare area (ploughed field, burnt ground, road, water), lie face down, cover with non-synthetic clothing or soil, breathe air close to the ground.

## Chemical incident / nuclear or radiation emergency
**Get in, stay in, tune in.**
1. Go inside the nearest solid building immediately. Basement or the middle of the building is best (more walls between you and outside).
2. Close windows and doors, switch off ventilation, air conditioning and extractor fans.
3. If you were outside during fallout: remove outer clothing (removes ~90% of contamination), bag it, shower or wash with soap and water, don't use conditioner.
4. Stay inside until authorities say otherwise (usually at least 24 h for fallout). Radiation from fallout drops fast: about 90% gone after 7 hours, 99% after 2 days.
5. Take **potassium iodide (KI) only when the authorities tell you to.** It only protects the thyroid against radioactive iodine. In NL it is distributed to under-40s and pregnant women near nuclear plants.
6. Eat stored food and water in sealed containers. Don't eat garden vegetables or drink rainwater until cleared.

## Earthquake (rare in NW Europe, some risk in Limburg/Rhineland)
Drop, cover (under a table), hold on. After: check for gas leaks (smell; don't use switches or flames), expect aftershocks.

## Civil unrest / conflict
Stay home and out of sight, keep a low profile, store water and food, agree a family meeting point and radio channel, keep documents and a grab bag ready by the door.

## Grab bag (per person)
Water 2 L, food for 3 days, this device + power bank, radio, torch, first aid kit, medicines (1 week), ID copies in a waterproof bag, cash, warm layer, rain poncho, foil blanket, whistle, knife/multitool, lighter, map of your area, phone charger, list of contacts on paper.
