# Making fire

Fire gives warmth, boils water, cooks, dries clothes, signals and lifts morale. Check wind and fire risk; dry summers in Europe bring wildfires. Clear a 1 m circle to bare earth, keep water or soil to put it out.

## Three kinds of fuel, gather all before lighting
1. **Tinder** (catches a spark): dry grass, birch bark (burns even damp, because of its oil), cattail fluff, wood shavings, cotton wool + Vaseline, char cloth, dry pine needles, "fatwood" (resin-rich pine).
2. **Kindling**: dry twigs from matchstick to thumb thickness. Take dead twigs **still on the tree** (they snap with a crack); ground wood is usually damp.
3. **Fuel**: wrist-thick and larger, dead and dry. Split wet logs: the inside is drier.
Gather **3× more** than you think you need.

## Lighting
- **Lighter/matches**: keep in a waterproof bag. Storm matches work in wind and rain.
- **Ferro rod**: scrape the rod (not the striker) hard and fast toward the tinder, or pull the rod back while holding the striker still so you don't scatter the tinder.
- **Battery**: 9 V battery against steel wool, or a AA with a gum-wrapper strip cut thin in the middle.
- **Lens**: magnifier, glasses, clear water-filled bag, focus sunlight to the smallest point on dark tinder.
- **Bow drill**: hard skill. Practise before you need it.

## Building
- **Teepee**: tinder ball, kindling leaned around it in a cone, light from the windward side. Best to start.
- **Log cabin**: kindling square around a teepee, burns steady for cooking.
- **Lean-to**: kindling leaned against a big log on the windward side, good in wind.
Feed slowly: add thicker wood only once the smaller pieces are burning well. Blow gently at the base.

## Wet conditions
Shave off wet bark, split sticks to get the dry core, make "feather sticks" (curls cut along a dry stick), build a platform of sticks so the fire is off wet ground, shelter it with your body or a tarp held high.

## Fire uses
- Reflector wall of logs or rocks behind the fire throws heat into your shelter.
- Hot stones (not from rivers: wet stones can explode) wrapped in cloth warm a bed.
- Boil water ([Purifying](page:water/purifying)). Charcoal from the fire works in water pre-filters.
- Signal: 3 fires in a triangle; green leaves/wet wood for white smoke in daylight.
