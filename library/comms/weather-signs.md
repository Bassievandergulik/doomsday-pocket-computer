# Reading the weather

The Weather tab forecasts from **air pressure change**. Combine it with what you see in the sky.

## Pressure (sea level)
| Reading | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Above 1020 hPa, steady or rising | Fair, settled. Cold nights and frost in winter under clear skies. |
| 1000–1020 hPa | Changeable |
| Below 1000 hPa | Unsettled, rain and wind likely |
| Fall of 1–3 hPa in 3 h | Weather worsening |
| Fall of **3.5–6 hPa in 3 h** | Strong wind and rain coming within hours |
| Fall of **more than 6 hPa in 3 h** | Gale or storm likely. Secure shelter, avoid woods (falling branches) |
| Fast rise after a low | Clearing but gusty, showers |

The barometer reads lower at higher altitude (about 1 hPa per 8 m). The app corrects to sea level using GPS altitude or `home_altitude_m`. If you carry it up or down a hill, the trend is disturbed: leave it at one height for a forecast.

## Clouds
- **Cirrus** (high wispy "mares' tails") thickening into a milky veil (**cirrostratus**, halo around sun/moon) → warm front, rain in 12–24 h.
- **Altostratus** grey sheet, sun like through frosted glass → rain within hours.
- **Nimbostratus** dark low layer → steady rain now.
- **Cumulus** small fluffy, fair weather. Growing tall by early afternoon → showers.
- **Cumulonimbus** anvil top, dark base → thunderstorm, hail, gusts. Lightning safety below.
- **Fog** on a clear calm morning usually burns off: fair day.

## Sayings that work
- "Red sky at night, shepherd's delight; red sky in the morning, shepherd's warning" (weather comes from the west in Europe).
- A ring around the moon: rain soon.
- Wind backing (turning anti-clockwise, e.g. W → SW → S): a low approaching, worse weather. Wind veering (clockwise): improving.
- Dew or frost in the morning: unlikely to rain that day.
- Smells stronger, sounds carry further, swallows flying low: humid air, rain likely.

## Lightning
Count seconds from flash to thunder, divide by 3 = distance in km. Under 10 km (30 s): take shelter. Inside a building or car is safe. Outdoors: avoid hilltops, lone trees, water, metal fences; crouch low on your pack with feet together, not lying flat; groups spread 5+ m apart. Wait 30 minutes after the last thunder.
