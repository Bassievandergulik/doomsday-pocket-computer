# Essential knots

| Knot | Use |
|---|---|
| **Bowline** | Fixed loop that won't slip or jam. Rescue loop, tie to a tree. |
| **Clove hitch** | Quick attach to a post or pole. Start of lashings. |
| **Taut-line hitch** | Adjustable tension for tent and tarp guy lines. |
| **Trucker's hitch** | Pull a line very tight (ridge lines, loads). |
| **Sheet bend** | Join two ropes, also of different thickness. |
| **Figure-eight** | Stopper knot; figure-eight loop for climbing. |
| **Square (reef) knot** | Tie bandages and slings. Not for joining ropes under load. |
| **Prusik** | Grips another rope, slides when loose; for climbing a rope or adjustable tarp attachments. |

## How to tie
- **Bowline**: make a small loop in the standing part ("rabbit hole"), the end comes **up** through the hole, **around** the back of the standing part ("round the tree"), and **back down** the hole. Pull tight. Leave a tail 10× rope thickness.
- **Clove hitch**: wrap around the pole, cross over the first turn, wrap again and tuck the end under the second wrap.
- **Taut-line hitch**: around the peg and back. With the end make two turns around the standing line **inside** the loop, then one turn **outside** (further up), tuck. Slide the knot to tension.
- **Trucker's hitch**: make a slip loop in the line halfway, pass the end around the anchor and back through the loop, pull down (2:1 pull), lock with two half hitches.
- **Sheet bend**: bight in the thicker rope; the thin end comes up through the bight, around behind both legs, and tucks under itself (not under the bight).
- **Square knot**: right over left and under, then left over right and under.
- **Two half hitches**: around the object, end around the standing part and through, repeat in the same direction.

## Lashing (shelter frames, stretchers)
Square lashing: clove hitch on one pole, wrap over-under around both poles 3–4 times, then 2–3 "frapping" turns between the poles to tighten, finish with a clove hitch.

## Improvised cordage
Inner bark of willow, lime (linden) or nettle stems, twisted reverse-wrap style. Paracord can be gutted: the 7 inner strands are thread or fishing line.
