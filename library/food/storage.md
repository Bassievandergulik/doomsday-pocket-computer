# Emergency food: stock, cooking, preserving

## Stock for 2 weeks per person (no fridge, no power)
| Food | Amount | Keeps |
|---|---|---|
| Rice, pasta, oats, couscous | 3 kg | 1–2 years |
| Canned beans, lentils, chickpeas | 10 cans | 2–5 years |
| Canned fish/meat | 7 cans | 3–5 years |
| Canned vegetables and fruit | 10 cans | 2–3 years |
| Crackers, crispbread, rusks (beschuit) | 2 packs | 1 year |
| Peanut butter, nuts | 1 kg | 1 year |
| Sugar, honey, jam, chocolate | 1 kg | long |
| Oil | 1 L | 1 year |
| Salt, stock cubes, spices | some | long |
| Milk powder / long-life milk | 1 kg / 4 L | 1 year |
| Multivitamins | 1 pack | 2 years |
Plus a can opener, camping stove with fuel, matches, lighters. Rotate: eat the oldest, buy new.

## Calories
Adults need about **2000–2500 kcal/day**, 3000+ for hard physical work in cold. Fat (9 kcal/g) is the densest store; carbs/protein 4 kcal/g. Rice 100 g dry ≈ 350 kcal, oats 100 g ≈ 380, peanut butter 100 g ≈ 600.

## Without power
- Fridge stays cold about **4 hours** if kept closed; a full freezer about **48 hours** (24 h half-full). Eat fridge food first, then freezer, then stores.
- When in doubt, throw it out: meat, fish, dairy, cooked rice above 7 °C for more than 2 hours.
- Cooking: camping stove **outdoors or with good ventilation** only. Never use a barbecue or charcoal indoors (carbon monoxide kills silently).
- Save fuel: soak beans and lentils, use a lid, use a "haybox" (bring to boil, then put the pot in a box packed with blankets/sleeping bag for 2–4 hours to finish cooking).

## Preserving
- **Drying**: thin slices of fruit, vegetables or lean meat, warm airy place or low oven/sun, until leathery. Store airtight.
- **Salting**: fish or meat packed in salt (about 1:3 salt:meat by weight), then dried.
- **Smoking**: cut fish or meat in thin strips (about 1 cm), salt them if you can (rub with salt or soak in strong salt water for an hour). Build a rack of green sticks about 1 m above a small fire of **hardwood** (oak, beech, alder, fruit tree; never pine, which makes it bitter), cover the rack with a tent of leafy branches or a tarp to keep the smoke in. Keep the fire low and smoky (add green wood or damp chips), not hot. Turn the strips now and then. After 12–24 hours they should be dry, dark and bend without breaking; store them dry and airy and eat within a few weeks.
- **Fermenting**: cabbage + 2% salt by weight, packed under its own brine (sauerkraut).

## Food safety
Bulging, leaking or badly dented cans: discard. Cook meat and fish thoroughly. Wild meat (boar especially) must be cooked through: trichinella risk.
