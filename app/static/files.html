Pocket survival computer: files

You are connected over the device's own WiFi. Open the device app here to use the map, guide and data on this computer.

Upload to the device

Drop files here, or
nav.db: map database from tools/build_navdb.py · map.mbtiles: satellite imagery from tools/build_imagery.py · .gpx: waypoints/routes planned on the computer (added to your waypoints) · .md: your own guide pages (Guide → My pages). Big files take a while: about 1–3 MB/s over the device's WiFi. Keep this page open until it says done.

Download from the device

Waypoints (GPX) Walked track (GPX) Weather log (CSV) Received weather charts and texts (ZIP) Full backup of your data
GPX opens in most map programs (OsmAnd, QGIS, Garmin BaseCamp, gpx.studio). The backup holds waypoints, radio presets, settings and the weather log.
