# Mushrooms

**Golden rule: don't eat a wild mushroom unless you are 100% sure of the species**, and never eat white-gilled mushrooms. Mushrooms give few calories: they are not worth the risk in a survival situation. Deadly poisoning often starts **6–24 hours later**, when it's too late to vomit; the person may seem better on day 2 while the liver is failing. Get to a hospital and bring a sample.

## Death cap
![Death cap](photo:death-cap.jpg)

*Amanita phalloides* · NL: groene knolamaniet · ES: oronja verde

- **Recognise**: olive-green to yellowish cap, **white gills**, a **ring** on the stem and a **bag-like cup (volva)** at the base, often hidden in the soil: always dig up the whole base
- **Effect**: causes most mushroom deaths worldwide. Half a cap can kill an adult

## Destroying angel
![Destroying angel](photo:destroying-angel.jpg)

*Amanita virosa* · NL: kleverige knolamaniet · ES: oronja cicuta

- **Recognise**: all white, white gills, ring and cup at the base
- **Effect**: deadly like the death cap; mistaken for young field mushrooms and puffballs

## Fly agaric
![Fly agaric](photo:fly-agaric.jpg)

*Amanita muscaria* · NL: vliegenzwam · ES: matamoscas

- **Recognise**: red cap with white warts, white gills, ring and warty base
- **Effect**: poisonous: confusion, vomiting, sometimes worse

## Chanterelle
![Chanterelle](photo:chanterelle.jpg)

*Cantharellus cibarius* · NL: hanenkam · ES: rebozuelo

- **Recognise**: egg-yolk yellow, smells of apricot, underside with blunt **forked ridges running down the stem** (not true thin gills), flesh white and stringy like chicken
- **Confuse with**: false chanterelle (orange, true thin gills) and the toxic jack-o'-lantern (grows in clusters on wood)

## Porcini / cep
![Porcini / cep](photo:porcini.jpg)

*Boletus edulis* · NL: eekhoorntjesbrood · ES: boleto / hongo calabaza

- **Recognise**: brown bun-like cap, **sponge** underneath (no gills) that is white to yellow-green, fat stem with a fine white net pattern near the top
- **Rule for sponge mushrooms**: avoid any with red or orange pores, or flesh that turns blue when cut, or a bitter taste

## Giant puffball
![Giant puffball](photo:giant-puffball.jpg)

*Calvatia gigantea* · NL: reuzenbovist · ES: bejín gigante

- **Recognise**: white ball from football size up, in meadows; cut it top to bottom: inside must be **pure white and even like marshmallow**
- **Danger**: a small 'puffball' that shows the outline of a cap and stem when cut is a young **deadly Amanita**

In doubt: don't. Plants are a much safer source of food: [Edible plants](page:plants/edible-plants).
